01 · Overview

What Sellquity is

Sellquity is reseller operations software designed to help users manage inventory, purchase costs, listings, marketplace activity, fees, payouts, expenses, and profit information.

This policy applies to the Sellquity website, Sellquity application, and related services operated under the Sellquity name.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sellquity does not sell or rent your personal information or marketplace account data to advertisers or data brokers.

02 · Information we collect

Information you provide to Sellquity

Account information

When you create or use a Sellquity account, we may collect information such as your name, email address, authentication information, account preferences, and workspace settings.

Reseller business information

Information you enter, upload, import, or create in Sellquity may include inventory records, SKUs, purchase prices, purchase sources, storage locations, listing information, sales, marketplace fees, shipping costs, payouts, expenses, notes, and related accounting data.

Technical information

We may process limited technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as IP address, browser or device information, session information, request logs, timestamps, and error information.

03 · Marketplace connections

Information received from eBay

If you connect an eBay account, Sellquity uses eBay's authorization system to access information that you authorize eBay to share with Sellquity. Depending on the features you use and permissions granted, this may include:

  • eBay seller account identifiers and basic account information;
  • inventory, offers, listings, listing status, price, and quantity data;
  • orders, line items, fulfillment status, and sales information;
  • fees, payouts, transaction records, shipping-label charges, and other financial activity; and
  • other marketplace information necessary to provide features you request.

Sellquity uses this information to provide reseller inventory, reconciliation, accounting, reporting, and automation features. Sellquity does not use your eBay data for advertising.

eBay access is authorized through OAuth. Sellquity stores authorization credentials server-side so the application can maintain an authorized connection without requiring you to sign in to eBay for every request. You may disconnect eBay from Sellquity at any time.

04 · Use of information

Why we process information

We use information collected through Sellquity to:

  • provide and operate the Sellquity service;
  • authenticate users and protect accounts;
  • organize and reconcile inventory, listings, sales, expenses, fees, payouts, and COGS;
  • connect authorized marketplace accounts and synchronize requested marketplace data;
  • produce reports, analytics, alerts, and reseller workflow recommendations;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the service;
  • communicate about account, security, service, or support matters; and
  • comply with legal, regulatory, marketplace, and platform obligations.
05 · Sharing

When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed only when reasonably necessary to operate Sellquity, including:

  • Service providers. Infrastructure, hosting, security, email, and other vendors may process limited information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.
  • Connected marketplaces. When you direct Sellquity to perform an authorized marketplace action, information necessary for that action may be exchanged with that marketplace.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or the rights and safety of others.
  • Business transfers. Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of all or part of the business, subject to applicable law.
06 · Security

How we protect information

Sellquity uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Marketplace authorization tokens are stored server-side and encrypted before database storage. Sellquity also uses authenticated sessions, access controls, secure HTTPS connections, and other security controls appropriate to the service.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we work to reduce risk and limit access to information to what is necessary.

07 · Retention & deletion

How long information is kept

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Sellquity, maintain your account and business records, comply with legal or marketplace obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce agreements.

When information is no longer reasonably needed, it may be deleted, anonymized, or retained only where required or permitted by law.

Marketplace account deletion requests

Sellquity maintains a public endpoint for marketplace account-deletion notifications. If a connected marketplace notifies Sellquity that a marketplace user has requested deletion of personal data, Sellquity will process applicable information in accordance with marketplace requirements and applicable law.

Sellquity account deletion

You may request deletion of your Sellquity account and associated personal information by contacting us at dave@sellquity.com. Certain business, accounting, security, or compliance records may be retained where legally required or reasonably necessary.

08 · Cookies & sessions

Keeping you signed in

Sellquity may use cookies or similar browser storage that are necessary for authentication, account security, session management, workspace selection, preferences, and core service functionality.

Sellquity does not use marketplace data to build advertising profiles.

09 · Your choices

Control over your information

Depending on the service features available to you, you may review or update account information, edit business records, disconnect a marketplace integration, request an export, or request account deletion.

You can also revoke Sellquity's access to a connected marketplace through that marketplace's account or authorization settings.

10 · Children

Sellquity is not directed to children

Sellquity is a business and reseller operations service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11 · Policy changes

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Sellquity evolves. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and may provide additional notice where appropriate.
